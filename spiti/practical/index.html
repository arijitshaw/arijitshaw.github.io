Emergency

Caution

The one rule for altitude

Normal: breathlessness on slopes, a headache that goes with paracetamol and water, broken sleep, low appetite. Not normal, and the answer is to go down, now, whatever the time: a headache painkillers do not touch, vomiting, confusion, unsteady walking, breathlessness at rest. Do not wait for morning to see whether it passes.

Source: Chapter 12.

Responsible travel

Respect

The short list

  • Fossils stay in the ground. They are protected under the Antiquities and Art Treasures Act, and Langza is a national geo-heritage site. Look, photograph, put it back. Chapter 12
  • Buy shawls from the weaver or a cooperative, and seabuckthorn from the local producers, so the money reaches the household that made it. Interlude Two
  • Stay in homestays and eat what the family eats. The income stays in the valley. Chapter 18
  • Refill, don’t buy water, and carry your rubbish out, especially from Chandratal, a Ramsar site. Chapter 14
  • Ask before photographing people, and keep a promise to send the picture. Never photograph a funeral. Manners
  • At a gompa: shoes off in shrine rooms, walk clockwise, no flash, leave a donation in the box. At cham, don’t walk into the dancing ground or film over other people’s heads. Interlude Eight
  • Don’t give children sweets, money or pens. Give to the school or the monastery, after asking what is needed. Manners
  • Don’t fly drones. Chapter 18

Source: the book.
