## Emergency {#emergency}

### Caution: the one rule for altitude

Normal: breathlessness on slopes, a headache that goes with paracetamol and water, broken sleep, low appetite. **Not normal, and the answer is to go down, now, whatever the time:** a headache painkillers do not touch, vomiting, confusion, unsteady walking, breathlessness at rest. Do not wait for morning to see whether it passes.

Source: [Chapter 12](../book/#ch-12).

## Responsible travel {#responsible}

### Respect: the short list

- **Fossils stay in the ground.** They are protected under the Antiquities and Art Treasures Act, and Langza is a national geo-heritage site. Look, photograph, put it back. [Chapter 12](../book/#ch-12)
- **Buy shawls from the weaver or a cooperative**, and seabuckthorn from the local producers, so the money reaches the household that made it. [Interlude Two](../book/#interlude-2)
- **Stay in homestays** and eat what the family eats. The income stays in the valley. [Chapter 18](../book/#ch-18)
- **Refill, don't buy water**, and carry your rubbish out, especially from Chandratal, a Ramsar site. [Chapter 14](../book/#ch-14)
- **Ask before photographing people**, and keep a promise to send the picture. Never photograph a funeral. [Manners](../book/#sidebar-manners)
- **At a gompa**: shoes off in shrine rooms, walk clockwise, no flash, leave a donation in the box. At cham, don't walk into the dancing ground or film over other people's heads. [Interlude Eight](../book/#interlude-8)
- **Don't give children sweets, money or pens.** Give to the school or the monastery, after asking what is needed. [Manners](../book/#sidebar-manners)
- **Don't fly drones.** [Chapter 18](../book/#ch-18)

Source: the book.
